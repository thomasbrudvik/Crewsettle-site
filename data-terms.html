Data terms & privacy

Pilot version · October 2026

I'm a commercial fisherman myself, and I take loyalty seriously. I know what a boat's numbers are worth, and that they stay on the boat.

Your catch, prices, expenses and crew pay are never sold, never used for advertising, and never shown to other boats, fish buyers, processors or anyone else in the industry. They are not used for fleet benchmarks, industry reports or to train AI.

They are processed only by the service providers listed below, to run CrewSettle for you. The only other exception: if disclosure is required by applicable law or a binding legal order. Then I will tell you first, unless the law forbids it.

Thomas Brudvik

Who is responsible

CrewSettle is currently operated by Thomas Brudvik in Norway. For privacy questions or requests, contact thomas@crewsettle.com.

  • Your boat's settlement and crew information: the vessel owner or operator decides what is entered and why, and is responsible for it (the "data controller"). CrewSettle only processes it on the boat's behalf, under a written data processing agreement that is part of the pilot agreement.
  • Your account, support emails and security information: CrewSettle is responsible for these.

What CrewSettle stores

  • Your email address, so you can sign in.
  • Your boat's name and the settlement numbers you enter: fish tickets (species, pounds, prices, deductions), expenses, lost gear, the crew split and each trip's dates.
  • Crew information you enter: name, role, shares, days on board, personal deductions, year-to-date totals and W-9 status (for example "received").
  • Basic technical records, such as sign-in times, to keep the service secure and working.

What CrewSettle does not ask for

  • CrewSettle does not ask for or intentionally store Social Security numbers or other tax ID numbers, bank or card details, or fishing locations.
  • A fish ticket can show more than the app needs, such as statistical areas or permit numbers. The app reads only species, weights, prices and deductions into your account.
  • Please cover or crop Social Security numbers and bank details before you scan a document.

Photos

When you scan a fish ticket or receipt, the photo is sent to Anthropic to be read. CrewSettle does not save the photo. Only the numbers you check and approve are saved to your account.

Who can see your data

  • Only the people who have access to your boat's account.
  • I can technically see the data, because I run the system. I only look at a boat's data when that boat asks me for help, and only what I need to help.

Why your data is used

  • Account and contact information: to provide CrewSettle and to talk to you about it.
  • Technical records: to keep the service secure and reliable.
  • Settlement and crew information: only to do what the boat uses CrewSettle for, following the boat's instructions.

CrewSettle processes account and contact information because it is necessary to provide the pilot service requested by the vessel. Support and security information is processed for CrewSettle’s legitimate interests in operating, protecting and improving the reliability of the service. The vessel owner or operator is responsible for determining the legal basis for processing crew information.

Companies that help run CrewSettle

These service providers process data on my behalf, only to deliver CrewSettle:

SupabaseSign-in and the app's database. Your saved data is stored on servers in Frankfurt, Germany.
Anthropic (Claude)Reads the photos you scan, through Anthropic's commercial API. Under Anthropic's standard policy, photos and results are normally deleted within 30 days, with exceptions for legal, security and usage-policy reasons. By default, Anthropic does not use inputs or outputs from its commercial API to train its models, and CrewSettle has not opted in to such use.
Google WorkspaceSends the sign-in emails and handles email to and from thomas@crewsettle.com.
GitHubHosts the crewsettle.com website. No settlement data is stored there, but GitHub may record technical visit data such as IP address and time.
TallyHosts the pilot application form and processes the information submitted through it, together with basic technical information needed to operate the form.

All data is sent over encrypted connections.

Data outside Europe

Your saved data is stored in Germany. Some providers, including Anthropic, Google and GitHub, are based in the United States. Scanned documents may be processed by Supabase and Anthropic in the United States or other locations described in their data processing agreements. Where required, these transfers are covered by the providers' data processing agreements, including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long data is kept

  • Your data is kept as long as your boat uses CrewSettle.
  • You can download your numbers to a spreadsheet at any time.
  • When the boat owner or operator asks me to delete the boat's data, it is deleted within 30 days, and I confirm by email when it is done. Residual copies may remain in service-provider backups until they are overwritten according to the provider’s backup schedule.
  • If your boat stops using CrewSettle after the pilot, I will ask whether you want an export, and then delete the data within 30 days.

Crew members

Crew information is entered by the vessel owner or operator, so requests about it should normally go to them. If a crew member contacts me directly, I will check who is asking, tell the vessel owner where appropriate, and help with seeing, correcting or deleting the information where the law allows. Some records, such as pay and tax records, the vessel owner may be required by law to keep.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, restrict or object to its use, and receive a portable copy. Email thomas@crewsettle.com. If you are not happy with how your data is handled, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).

Changes

If these terms change materially, I will email you before the change takes effect.
