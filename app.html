Pay slips

Details for your accountant

Rounding rule: every amount is worked in whole cents. Each crew member first gets their share of the crew pool rounded down to the cent. The cents left over go one at a time to the crew members with the largest dropped fractions, with ties going in crew-list order. The individual shares therefore always add up to the crew pool exactly.

Export, save and next trip

For your accountant

Draft exports, not yet tested in QuickBooks. Import only into a test file or a copy of the books, never into live books, until your accountant has checked the result.

QuickBooks account names and options

The journal is balanced: crew shares are debited, and net pay, advances and deductions are credited, so debits equal credits. Account names must match the accounts in the QuickBooks file exactly.

Backup

Your work is saved to your CrewSettle account automatically. If you want an extra copy, you can download a backup file. The file contains crew names and pay, so store and share it securely. Once downloaded, it is your responsibility.

Next trip

When this settlement is final: each crew member's share is added to their year-to-date totals, and the fish tickets, expenses, lost gear and personal deductions are cleared for the next trip. The crew list stays, so you only change who is on board.

Closed trips

Each closed trip is kept exactly as it was settled: catch, expenses, shares and pay slips. It cannot be changed.

1099 preparation report

Tax year 2026

Year-end 1099 preparation report — review with your accountant before filing. The app adds up every settlement through the year and prepares the numbers your accountant needs. It does not file anything with the IRS and does not store Social Security numbers. Your accountant or a tax service collects the W-9s and files the 1099-MISC forms.

How shared expenses, personal deductions, advances and later price adjustments affect the Box 5 amount must be confirmed by a US fisheries accountant.
